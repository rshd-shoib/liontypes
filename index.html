About LionTypes

LionTypes is a typing test built around a reactive 3D lion avatar — its mane, eyes, and posture respond live to your speed, streak, and mistakes, rendered in WebGL with hand-written GLSL shaders. Under the hood it tracks words-per-minute, accuracy, and consistency the same way most typing tests do, then adds a real leaderboard of actual runs submitted from this site.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Words per minute is calculated as (correctly typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed — the standard typing-test convention, where one "word" is counted as 5 characters regardless of actual word length.

How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is the percentage of keystrokes that were correct on the first try, out of all keystrokes typed during the test — including characters you later corrected.

What's the difference between time, words, quote, and zen mode?

Time gives you a fixed duration and counts how much you type. Words gives you a fixed word count and times how long it takes. Quote has you type a real quote start to finish. Zen has no limit — type for as long as you want, then stop whenever.

Is the leaderboard real people?

The "on this site" leaderboard is real — every entry is an actual finished run submitted from this site, with basic server-side checks against obviously spoofed scores. The other reference boards are sourced from public data on other typing sites and documented world records, not LionTypes runs, and are labeled as such.

Is LionTypes free?

Yes — no account, no paywall, no ads.
