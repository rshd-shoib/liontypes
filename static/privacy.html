Privacy

Last updated August 2026

LionTypes is a typing test. This page covers, plainly, what the site collects and why.

Leaderboard submissions

When you finish a typing test, your result (the nickname you choose, WPM, accuracy, mode, and amount) is sent to our server and stored so it can appear on the site's leaderboard. Your IP address is stored alongside it, used only to apply a basic rate limit against spam submissions — it is never shown publicly and never included in leaderboard results.

Local storage

Your theme, sound, and chosen nickname preferences, plus your own test history, are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so they persist between visits on that device. This data never leaves your browser and isn't something we can see or access.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up is required or offered.
  • No advertising, and no ad trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of any data with third parties.

Infrastructure

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process standard connection metadata (like IP address) as part of ordinary request handling and security — this is infrastructure-level logging common to virtually any website, governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy, not something LionTypes configures or has separate access to.

Contact

Questions about this page can be sent to the site owner via the contact details listed on liontypes.in.
